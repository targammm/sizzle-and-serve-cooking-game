Sizzle & Serve — 3 stations, 12 recipes, 5 ingredients, combo scoring. A busy little kitchen game: take ingredients from the counter, chop them or grill the patty, build the dish on a plate, and serve it before the order timer runs out. Orders stack up, the combo grows with every serve in time, and the shift ends after 3 minutes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD moves the chef, click a bin to take an ingredient, click CHOP / GRILL / PLATE / SERVE to work the stations, or press E near a station. Keys 1–5 take ingredients directly.
